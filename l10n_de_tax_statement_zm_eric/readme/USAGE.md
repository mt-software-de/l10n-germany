1.  Create a German VAT Statement, which computes its ZM lines, and
    check them.
2.  *Approve* sends the ZM to the BZSt along with the statement to the
    tax office, with the same certificate and PIN. ERiC validates the
    ZM before the statement is sent. The message of the approval has
    the transfer ticket and the transmission protocol of the ZM too.
3.  If ELSTER rejects the ZM, the statement stays approved and the message
    of the approval has the error: correct the cause and send the ZM with
    *Send ZM to the BZSt*.

The ZM covers the period of the statement: a month is sent as monthly ZM,
a quarter as quarterly ZM. A corrected statement sends a corrected ZM
(Meldeart 11).
