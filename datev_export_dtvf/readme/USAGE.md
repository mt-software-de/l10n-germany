To use this module, you need to:

1.  Go to Invoicing / Reporting / DATEV export
2.  Create an export, choose a date range to use as fiscal year, and
    ranges to export
3.  Click `Generate`

To send the documents of your journal entries to DATEV too, activate
`Export Attachments` before you click `Generate`. The bookings of journal
entries with a main attachment then link to it, and you get a second archive
with those attachments and a `document.xml`. Upload this archive to DATEV
Unternehmen online separately, for example using DATEV Belegtransfer.
