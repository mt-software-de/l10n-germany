This module sends the *Zusammenfassende Meldung* (ZM) of the German VAT
Statement to the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) with ERiC, the
library of the tax administration. It glues *German VAT Statement
Extension* and *German VAT Statement ERiC* and is installed with both of
them.

It sends the ZM as the data type ``ZMDO`` with the certificate of Mein
ELSTER of the company, a BZSt-Nummer isn't needed.

Usage
=====

1. Create a German VAT Statement, which computes its ZM lines, and check
   them.
2. *Approve* sends the ZM to the BZSt along with the statement to the
   tax office, with the same certificate and PIN. ERiC validates the ZM
   before the statement is sent. The message of the approval has the
   transfer ticket and the transmission protocol of the ZM too.
3. If ELSTER rejects the ZM, the statement stays approved and the
   message of the approval has the error: correct the cause and send the
   ZM with *Send ZM to the BZSt*.

The ZM covers the period of the statement: a month is sent as monthly
ZM, a quarter as quarterly ZM. A corrected statement sends a corrected
ZM (Meldeart 11).

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The ZM can only cover the period of the statement: a company which
  declares its VAT quarterly but its ZM monthly can't send it yet.
- Triangular transactions (Dreiecksgeschäfte, Umsatzart D) aren't
  supported.
- A corrected ZM sends all of its lines again, not only the corrected
  ones.
- The indications of the monthly ZM (anzeige, widerruf) can't be set.
