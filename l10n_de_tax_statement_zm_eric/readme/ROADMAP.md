- The ZM can only cover the period of the statement: a company which
  declares its VAT quarterly but its ZM monthly can't send it yet.
- Triangular transactions (Dreiecksgeschäfte, Umsatzart D) aren't
  supported.
- A corrected ZM sends all of its lines again, not only the corrected
  ones.
- The indications of the monthly ZM (anzeige, widerruf) can't be set.
